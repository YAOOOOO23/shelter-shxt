Gathering art — built-in image_gen edits and generation.
All outputs are shipped in assets/ and referenced by gathering.js.
Actions: 8 x 128px RGBA frames, one shared scale and foot baseline per action.
Fixed horizontal body anchors preserve torso position when a raised tool changes the image bounds.
Targets: tree, ore rock, logs, creek water; 4 x 256px RGBA tiles.
Original approved character: sprites.png crop (404,417,659,706).
Normalization previews are kept outside the repository in the session visualization workspace.
